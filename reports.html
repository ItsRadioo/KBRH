Program Outcomes

Program success rate = successful completions ÷ (successful completions + involuntary removals). Voluntary early departures are neutral, not failures. Historic/legacy, other, medical, transfer and legal outcomes are not included in this rate or the outcome charts. Records remain preserved in resident history.
