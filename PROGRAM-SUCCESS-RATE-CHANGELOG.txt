KBRH Program Success Rate update

Formula: Completed program exits / all actual program exits in the reporting date range.
Includes voluntary, involuntary, medical, transfer, legal, other and unclassified archived discharges in denominator.
Excludes active residents and administrative returns to waitlist.
Applies to Outcome Reports KPI, monthly report, CSV export and Dashboard KPI.
Historical records with unknown outcome count as exits but not completions. Review historical classifications for accuracy.
